What it reads:

  • a release as Ambrosia shipped it: EV_Nova_1.0.10.sit, 1.0.8 or 1.0.2;
  • the .ndat files from 1.1.1's Nova Files folder, all selected at once;
  • the community's builds and their .rez files, or the zip they come in;
  • a MacBinary or BinHex copy of each data file;
  • plug-ins, as .rez, .sit or .zip, opened with a game or after it.

A file picked straight out of a classic Mac folder arrives without its resource fork, which is where the game keeps everything, so it cannot be read that way.

With no copy to hand, read the one archive.org keeps: 1.0.10 as Ambrosia shipped it, 96 MB, fetched when you ask.
