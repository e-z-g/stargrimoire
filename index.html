What the page is built from

  • The EV Nova Resource Bible, by Matt Burch, as reformatted by ReinierK, and Ambrosia's ResEdit templates: the records' fields, units and flags.
  • Grimoire: the readers for Mac archives, resource forks and pictures, whose StuffIt reader comes from Ben Letchford's stuffit-rs, after Dag Ågren's The Unarchiver.
  • evnova-decomp, by Lancelot de Ferrière: the Community Edition's function names, which helped read its program alongside the Mac one.
  • ResForge, by Andrew Simmonds: how a ship's sprite layers are blended.
  • The subway map, after Gansner, Koren and North; Stott, Rodgers, Martinez-Ovando and Walker; and Bast, Brosi and Storandt. Its names, after Been, Daiches and Yap; Been, Nöllenburg, Poon and Wolff; and Christensen, Marks and Shieber. Its colours for jumps, Anton Mikhailov's turbo.

Everyone named here is credited for work StarGrimoire draws on. None of them was involved in making it or has endorsed it, and its mistakes are its maintainer's.

What it reads:

  • a release as Ambrosia shipped it: EV_Nova_1.0.10.sit, 1.0.8 or 1.0.2;
  • 1.1.1's disk image, EV_Nova_1.1.1_for_Mac_OS_X.dmg, or the .ndat files from its Nova Files folder, all selected at once;
  • the community's builds and their .rez files, or the zip they come in;
  • a MacBinary or BinHex copy of each data file;
  • plug-ins, as .rez, .sit or .zip, opened with a game or after it.

A file picked straight out of a classic Mac folder comes without its resource fork, and that is where all of the game's data is, so it cannot be read that way.

With no copy to hand, read the one on archive.org: 1.0.10 as Ambrosia shipped it, 96 MB, fetched when you ask.
